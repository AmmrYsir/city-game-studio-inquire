Section 01

How your review score is decided

Four magazines review every release. Their notes are built from four checks — here they are in order of importance, with how many points each is worth.

🎯
MULTIPLIER

Genre + Platform + Audience match

Three pairings are graded and multiplied: Excellent ×1.10Good ×1.05 OK ×1.00Poor ×0.85
genre↔platform · theme↔genre · platform↔audience. One Poor pairing silently costs 15% — the in-game match tips are real data, trust them.

⚖️
84 PTS MAX

Capacity balance — the hidden test

Each genre has a secret shopping list (Section 02). Reviewers grade the proportions of your spending across Design, Development and Polish (28 points each). Match the ratios for full marks; lopsided allocation loses up to half and draws "too much X / not enough X" complaints.

⏱️
20 PTS MAX

Development duration

Finish inside the healthy window for your size. Every week past the max steals points back linearly; rushing under the minimum also scores low.

🔁
−25% RISK

Idea repetition

Same theme + genre combo within 6–12 months (difficulty-dependent) applies up to a ×0.75 penalty. Alternate themes or wait it out.

🎲

The magazine split

Your absolute note is dealt to 4 magazines, each rolled ±10%. The final score is the average. Notes above 100 are floored to multiples of 10.

💡
Score caps by size: Indie 85 · Normal 90 · Big 95 · AAA 100. An oversized custom engine for the platform lowers even that cap — keep engines lean.

Section 03

Pricing — where the real money is

Price has its own dedicated math, and it's generous — if you stay under the max. Cross it and a penalty bites hard.

✅At or below max price

Volume scales as max_price ÷ your_price. Halving the price roughly doubles copies sold. Cheap also feeds fanbase growth — great for building an audience.

⛔Above max price

Penalty = malus × limit ÷ price. AAA at 150 instead of 120 loses ~35% volume. The higher the malus, the harder greed is punished on small sizes.

Section 04

Marketing & hype

Hype is a launch multiplier stacked on your first months of sales. Four sources feed it:

📺Campaigns

The campaign ratio multiplies your last month's unit sales into extra launch hype. Bigger sizes get longer campaign windows.

🤝Publishers & announcements

Self-published starts at ×0.5 hype; a publisher or strong announcement ramps it up to ×4 — an 8× difference.

🎪Conventions & events

Add a static hype bonus on top of your base — cheap wins when timed before release.

👔Marketing Manager

This employee multiplies the entire hype total by their personal bonus. Hire one before any big launch.

Section 05

How sales play out month by month

Every in-game month, each published game sells a slice of its platform's userbase. Three forces decide how big that slice is.

📊1 · Review score is sales dynamite

The score enters sales raised to a huge power (7.5 for indie up to 14 for AAA) — small score gains explode into big sales gains:

👥2 · Fanbase & freshness

You share the platform's player base with every other game released there — fewer competitors = bigger slice. Fanbase adds direct sales; both fade over the months. Games scoring below fan expectations lose fans monthly instead.

😱3 · Things that hurt

  • Piracy — cracked games sell ⅓ (old platforms lack protection)
  • Royalties — publisher cut + engine owner's cut
  • Taxes — monthly; an accountant lowers them

Section 07

Console strategy cheat-sheet

📜

Licenses: budget upfront

Consoles need a dev license before you can build for them — ~1,000 for gen-1 relics up to 4,000,000 for the newest generation (median ≈ 25,000). Too poor? The game locks them as "too expensive".

💪

Newer consoles demand more points

Platform power (CPU/GPU) sets how many capacity points a game needs to compete. The same title that ruled gen-3 may cap out low on gen-8 without the points behind it.

📦

Ports: the cheap money printer

Ports reuse your original capacity points, scaled by platform distance: backwards-compatible ≈ 25% extra points needed · same-gen 37–44% · next-gen 48–51%. The score is re-graded on the new platform's genre/audience matches — pick targets where your genre is Excellent.

🛡️

Piracy zones

Retro and unprotected platforms can get your games cracked — ⅓ sales while infected. Prioritize protected platforms for your big releases; treat old consoles as cheap portfolio fillers.

🌐

Online platforms (40 of 88)

Online unlocks digital-store sales and subscription income from online games — but servers cost money and cap concurrency. Retro platforms are physical-sales only.

🧠
The loop: license cheap old consoles while their libraries are small → dominate their sales share → port the back catalog to backwards-compatible new machines (nearly free) → keep mature content off handhelds → ride each new generation early with lean engines and full campaigns sized to the platform.
