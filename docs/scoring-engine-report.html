City Game Studio - Scoring Engine & Sales Model Extracted from the game's compiled GDScript (Godot 2.1 bytecode, AES-decrypted). Values are exact constants from the shipped game.

How a game's review score is computed - when development finishes, the game's capacity points are compared against genre expectations, then multiplied through theme/platform matching, duration, platform library, and idea-repetition factors, and finally split into 4 magazine notes with ±10% randomness. The resulting computed_note then drives monthly sales, fanbase and awards.

1 · The scoring pipeline

1
Engine penalty → max_note cap. Each game size has a hard cap (indie 85 · normal 90 · big 95 · aaa 100). A too-heavy custom engine for the platform lowers this cap: 
note_ratio = clamp( size_ratio × (1 − used_gpu_cpu / required_gpu_cpu), −size_ratio/4, size_ratio )
max_note   = size.max_note − note_ratio
Too heavy (ratio > 1.5) triggers a negative review comment; very light (< 0.5) a positive one.
2
Capacity-point scoring (the core). The three dev phases - design, development, polish - are each scored out of 28 points (max total 84). For every capacity in a phase: 
note_per_item = 28 / (#capacities in phase)
expected      = genre expectation value (matrix below)   ← per genre, phase & capacity
game_share    = your_points[key] / Σ your_points_in_phase
expected_share= expected[key]    / Σ expected_in_phase
delta         = |expected_share − game_share| × capacity_delta_time   (difficulty: 1.085–1.667)
note          = note_per_item × (expected_share − clamp(delta, 0, expected_share)) / expected_share
Then a rebalance multiplier compares each capacity's share vs expectation: 
ratio = clamp(0.1 + share_you / share_expected, …)
note ×= lerp(0.5, 1.0, ratio)
Review comments: |your − expected| > 20 → “too much X”; ≤ 5 → “good X”; share ratio ≤ 0.6 → “not enough”; ≥ 0.8 → “good”.
3
Required points baseline. Points needed to “fill the gauge” per size: 
points = ideal_weeks × staff × 0.4671 × week_productivity × productivity × point_to_produce_ratio
productivity = 1.15 if a producer is required, else 1.0 · point_to_produce_ratio: 0.2826 (easy) … 3.0 (hard)
4
Theme / genre / platform / audience match multipliers. Three independent match lookups multiply the note (POOR 0.85 · OK 1.00 · GOOD 1.05 · EXCELLENT 1.10): genre↔platform preferred genres, theme↔genre, platform↔target audience.
5
Duration factor (contributes up to 20 points, min 5): 
note = weeks × 20 / min_weeks
if weeks > max_weeks: note = 20 − (weeks − max_weeks) × 20 / min_weeks
duration_note = clamp(note, 5, 20);  note ×= duration_note / 20
6
Platform library. Your total points are compared to the best same-size, same-genre game already on the platform (floored at your own baseline): 
note_ratio = clamp(your_points / rival_points, capacity_min_ratio, max_bonus)
absolute_note ×= lerp(size.platform_note_min_ratio, 1.0, note_ratio)
7
Idea repetition. Same theme+genre combo within the cooldown window (6 / 8 / 12 months by difficulty) scales the note: 
absolute_note ×= clamp(months_since / min_delay, 0.75, 1.0)
8
Magazine split. 4 magazines, each ±10% random jitter; notes above 100 are floored to multiples of 10. Final score = mean of the 4. 
for i in 4: note[i] = int(absolute_note × rand(0.9, 1.1));  if >100: note -= note % 10
computed_note = int(mean(notes))

5 · Monthly sales model

Runs every in-game month for every published game (own, indie/contract, remake, port, DLC).

A
Base pool. Platform userbase, discounted by how long the game has been on the market and how many games compete on the platform: 
cycle_ratio   = (adjusted_cycle − 2) / (adjusted_cycle + 2)      ← ramps up, saturates at 1
max_unit_sold = platform_userbase × cycle_ratio
competing     = min(1, size.sales.times × (#games_on_platform ^ 1.1))   ← pow_factor 1.1

base_pool = clamp(max_unit_sold, max_sales) × (theme_trend / 50)
saturation = 1 − 1/(1 + sqrt(base_pool)/competing)
B
Review-note shaping. The score enters sales as a power curve - this is why an 85 is dramatically better than a 70: 
note = min(0.25, computed_note / 100) ^ size.note_pow_factor      ← 7.5 (indie) … 14 (aaa)
C
Fanbase input. 
fan_ratio = (fanbase / (cycle + 1)) × size.fan_ratio        ← 0.55 … 1.1
fanbase_gate = clamp((fanbase + 1) / required_fanbase, fan_min_ratio, 1.0)
required_fanbase grows by (1 + score_diff) when the game underperforms its threshold (95/95/90/90)
D
Combine: 
unit_sold = (base_pool × saturation + fan_ratio) × note
unit_sold = unit_sold / (cycle + cycle_boost) × price_ratio × fanbase_gate × sales_ratio / discount_ratio
Price ratio (sell cheap → volume): 
r = price / size.price.max
ratio = max_price/price if r < 1 else size.price.malus × size.price.limit / price
Hype/marketing add-on (retained users × sell-bonus × 10, plus campaign ratio × last month's units, × marketing-manager employee bonus), clamped to platform size, added to units.
E
Retention & online games. Repeat customers decay logarithmically: 
retained = last_units × max_retention × note / (log(cycle + 10) + cycle_boost) × retention_ratio
Online games charge retained × monthly_price per month; server capacity caps income.
F
Income waterfall (deductions in order): 
income = ceil(units × price × (1 − publisher_royalty%))
       + online sub income + DLC/purchase income
− concurrent/publisher royalty  − engine royalties (if engine not owned)
− studio-director profit bonus  − taxes ((taxes_ratio + state_taxes) × accountant bonus)
Piracy: if the game gets cracked, units / DS units / retention / fanbase-gain ÷ 3.
G
Fanbase gain/loss. 
threshold = clamp(fanbase / fanbase_ctrl_threshold, 0.5, 1) × 100
gain = units × (computed_note − threshold)/100 × size.fan_gain
if gain < 0: gain = min(fanbase × (note − threshold)/500, units × −2.5)   ← fans leave
Below-expectation games actively bleed fans.
H
DLC. Attach-rate ratio shrinks as more DLCs pile up: 
attach_ratio = 1 + 2(n_dlc − 1)² / (n_dlc + 1)
units = base-game attach × attach_rate / (100 × attach_ratio); income = units × dlc_price

6 · Cheat-sheet: what actually moves the needle

  • Match the genre matrix - allocation distribution is scored, not volume. The rebalance multiplier can halve your capacity note (lerp 0.5).
  • Don't overrun duration - past max weeks every extra week linearly eats the 20-point duration bonus.
  • Don't repeat combos - same theme+genre within 6–12 months costs up to 25% of the score.
  • 85 vs 70 matters enormously - note^7.5…14 power curve means a 5-point note difference swings sales by tens of percent.
  • Price at max is punishing - above average price the malus (0.5–0.8) × limit/price ratio kicks in; cheap prices get a ≥1.1 volume ratio.
  • Fanbase gates sales - underperforming self-published games raise their own fanbase requirement (score_diff feedback loop).
  • AAA cap is 100, indie is 85 - engine weight can lower even that cap.
